### 2.1 Mechanistic & Licensing Notes on the New 2025–2026 Mega-Atlases

1. **Why `X-Atlas/Orion` (`8.0M` cells, `18,903` genes) Solves the Non-Essential Ferroptosis Gene Blind Spot:**  
   In `Replogle et al. (2022)`, only `K562` leukemia received a `9,866`-gene screen (expressed genes in myeloid leukemia), whereas `RPE1` received only `2,393` essential genes—missing lineage-restricted lipid/iron/antioxidant enzymes poorly expressed in `K562` (`AIFM2`/`FSP1`, `MBOAT1`, `MBOAT2`, `PROM2`, `HILPDA`, `EPAS1`/`HIF2A`, `ALOX15`, `GCH1`, `CYP4F2`, `VKORC1L1`). `X-Atlas/Orion` (`Huang et al., 2025` [`10.1101/2025.06.11.659105`]) screens **all `18,903` human protein-coding genes** in an epithelial colorectal carcinoma line (`HCT116`, `75.4%` median knockdown) and an embryonic kidney line (`HEK293T`, `51.5%` median knockdown) at `~16,000` UMIs/cell, while recording guide UMI counts per cell to enable **continuous gene-dosage response modeling** inside `FerroFlux-FM` Stage 1.
2. **Why `Parse-10M` (`90` Cytokines) + `Jiang 2025` (`PIE_jiang`) + `Frangieh 2021` (`GSE153056`) Unlock Immunometabolic Ferroptosis:**  
   Effector $\text{CD8}^+$ T-cell-derived **`IFN-γ`** activates `JAK1/2–STAT1` (`IRF1`) to transcriptionally repress system $x_c^-$ (`SLC7A11`/`SLC3A2`) and upregulate `ACSL4` in tumor cells, triggering immunogenic ferroptosis (`Wang et al., Nature 2019` [`10.1038/s41586-019-1170-y`]), whereas `CSF1`/`TGF-β`/`IL-10` polarize ferroptosis-resistant M2/TAM myeloid niches—precisely the axis reprogrammed by Bradbury Lab's `C′ dots` (`De Leon et al., Nat. Nanotechnol. 2026` [`10.1038/s41565-025-02083-z`]; `Siddiqui et al., Cancer Res. 2026` [`10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-25-4954`]). Together, `Parse-10M` (`~9.69M` primary human immune cells × `90` cytokines), `PIE_jiang` (`516,897` epithelial/heme cells incl. `BxPC-3` & `HAP-1` under `IFN-γ`/`IFN-β`/`TNF-α`/`TGF-β` × pathway KOs), and `Frangieh 2021` (`218,331` patient melanoma cells × `248` KOs under `IFN-γ` and TIL attack) give `FerroFlux-FM` complete causal observability across **both sides of the immune–tumor synapse**.

### 3.1 Why Clinical ICB Cohorts Are Essential for Zero-Shot Bradbury Lab Translation

A major translational paradox in oncology is that **dedifferentiated mesenchymal / `IPRES` melanomas (`Hugo 2016`), `PTEN`-null myeloid-suppressive GBMs (`Zhao 2019`, `Cloughesy 2019`), and `VHL`-null/`NRF2`-dysregulated ccRCCs (`Braun 2020`, `JAVELIN Renal 101`) resist anti-PD-1 monotherapy**, yet acquire profound **PUFA-phospholipid / iron-addicted vulnerability to ferroptosis** (`Tsoi et al., Cancer Cell 2018` [`10.1016/j.ccell.2018.03.017`]; `Zou et al., Nat. Commun. 2019` [`10.1038/s41467-019-09277-9`]). Pretraining/evaluating `FerroFlux-FM` across these `~2,275` immediately open bulk ICB biopsies (`3,098` total incl. `IMmotion151`) and `>225,000` clinical scRNA-seq cells allows the model to:
1. Learn the exact *in vivo* transcriptional-metabolic manifold induced by PD-1/PD-L1 blockade (`Riaz 2017` `GSE91061` pre $\to$ on-tx; `Cloughesy 2019` `GSE121810` neoadjuvant vs control);
2. Deconvolve tumor-cell ferroptosis vulnerability ($\Phi_{\text{ferro}}^{\text{tumor}}$) from **immune-cell ferroptosis collateral damage** ($\Phi_{\text{ferro}}^{\text{CD8+ T}}$ vs $\Phi_{\text{ferro}}^{\text{TAM/MDSC}}$ in `Bi 2021`, `Krishna 2021`, `Sade-Feldman 2018`), directly predicting why ultrasmall `<8 nm` `C′ dots` convert cold/myeloid-suppressive tumors into checkpoint-responsive states without ablating anti-tumor $\text{CD8}^+$ T cells (`De Leon et al. 2026`; `Siddiqui et al. 2026`).
