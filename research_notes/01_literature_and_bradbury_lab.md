## Executive Summary: Strategic Thesis for a *Nature*/*Science*-Caliber Ferroptosis Foundation Model

1. **Why PI Michelle S. Bradbury's Lab Is the Ideal Downstream Validation Partner:** Over the past decade (2016–2026), **Dr. Michelle S. Bradbury** (Professor of Pharmacology [Primary, 2026–], Professor of Neuroscience & Radiology in Radiation Oncology, Weill Cornell Medicine; Director of Intraoperative Imaging, Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center; PI of **NCI R01 CA253658-05 [2023–2027]**, *"Ultrasmall particle-based solutions for inducing ferroptosis and improving anti-tumor immune responses in cancer"*) — in close collaboration with **Ulrich Wiesner** (Cornell University) and **Michael Overholtzer** (MSKCC) — established that clinically translated, sub-8 nm renally clearable core–shell silica nanoparticles (**Cornell prime dots / C′ dots**) possess *intrinsic, drug-free ferroptosis-inducing and tumor-microenvironment (TME)-reprogramming activity* ([Kim et al., *Nat. Nanotechnol.* 2016](https://doi.org/10.1038/nnano.2016.164); [Riegman et al., *Nat. Cell Biol.* 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41556-020-0565-1); [De Leon et al., *Nat. Nanotechnol.* 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41565-025-02083-z); [Das et al., *Dev. Crucially, C′ dot ferroptosis is **conditionally synthetic-lethal**: nanoparticles are non-toxic in nutrient-replete normal cells and clear rapidly through the kidneys without systemic toxicity, yet trigger lysosomal iron mobilization, glutathione (GSH) collapse, wave-like multicellular lipid peroxidation, and STING/TLR-driven antitumor immunity in metabolically stressed solid tumors.
2. **Why Existing Ferroptosis ML Models (2019–2026) Fail:** Published ferroptosis ML models — dominated by TCGA LASSO/XGBoost "ferroptosis risk scores," static FerrDb gene-set scoring, cell-agnostic small-molecule QSAR classifiers, droplet scRNA-seq module scores, and surrogate-trained H&E predictors — suffer from **six fatal flaws**, foremost the fallacy that steady-state mRNA abundance equals membrane lipid peroxidation flux. None model membrane polyunsaturated vs. monounsaturated phospholipid stoichiometry (`di-PUFA-PL` vs. `MUFA-PL`), subcellular labile $\text{Fe}^{2+}$ and polyamine buffering, radical-trapping antioxidant (RTA) pools ($\text{CoQ}_{10}\text{H}_2$, vitamin K hydroquinone, $\text{BH}_4$, 7-dehydrocholesterol), UGA selenocysteine translational stalling on *GPX4* mRNA, multicellular spatial trigger waves, or unseen nanoparticle/nutrient perturbations.
3. **What a True Mechanistic Foundation Model Must Capture:** Breakthroughs from 2024–2026 in *Nature*, *Science*, and *Cell* prove that ferroptosis is governed by a **multi-compartment biophysical-metabolic reaction-diffusion system** coupling: (i) peroxidizable membrane substrates (`PC-PUFA2`, `PE-AA/AdA`, peroxisomal ether plasmalogens vs. `MBOAT1/2`-remodeled `MUFA-PLs`); (ii) compartmentalized iron catalysis (lysosomal $\text{Fe}^{2+}$ activation, lysosome rupture switches, ER–mitochondria contact hotspots, and spermine/polyamine iron buffering); (iii) four orthogonal antioxidant shields (`SLC7A11–GSH–GPX4`, `FSP1–CoQ10/VK`, `GCH1–BH4`, and distal sterol `7-DHC` controlled by `EBP`/`DHCR7`); (iv) spatial **ROS trigger waves** (~5.5 $\mu\text{m/min}$) sculpted by nematic tissue self-organization; and (v) **cell-type therapeutic asymmetry** (killing malignant and myeloid-suppressive cells while sparing $\text{CD8}^+$ TILs and CAR-T cells).

## 1. Deep Profile of PI Michelle S. Bradbury's Ferroptosis & C′ Dot Research Program (MSKCC / Weill Cornell, 2011–2026)

### 1.1 Platform Architecture: Cornell Prime Dots (C′ Dots) & Target-or-Clear Nanotheranostics
Cornell prime dots (**C′ dots**) are water-synthesized, ultrasmall (**5.5–7.5 nm hydrodynamic diameter**; strictly below the ~8–10 nm glomerular filtration threshold) fluorescent core–shell silica nanoparticles covalently encapsulating near-infrared dyes (Cy5, CW800) inside an ~3–4 nm silica core and sterically stabilized by a neutral oligo-poly(ethylene glycol) (PEG) shell ([Benezra et al., *J. Clin. Invest.* 2011](https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI45600); [Ma/Chen et al., *Chem. Mater.* 2017](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemmater.7b03033)). Unlike conventional >20–100 nm nanomedicines trapped by hepatic/splenic reticuloendothelial macrophages, C′ dots obey a **"target-or-clear"** pharmacokinetic paradigm ([Chen et al., *Chem. Mater.* 2017](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemmater.7b02567); [Joyce et al., *Nat. Nanotechnol.* 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41565-024-01754-7); [Sofias et al., *Nat. Rev. Cancer* 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41568-026-00983-3)) and have advanced across multiple FDA IND-approved human clinical trials (`NCT01266096` $^{124}\text{I-cRGDY}$ PET in melanoma/malignant brain tumors, [Phillips et al., *Sci. Transl. Med.* 2014](https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.3009524); `NCT02106598` Phase 1/2 `cRGDY–PEG–Cy5.5–C′ dots` in head & neck melanoma sentinel lymph node biopsy, [Zanoni et al., *JAMA Netw. Open* 2021](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2021.1936); `NCT03465618` Phase 1 $^{89}\text{Zr-DFO-cRGDY-C′ dots}$ in malignant brain tumors; and `NCT04167969` recruiting Phase 1 PSMA-targeted C′ dots in prostate cancer, PI Bradbury — whereas commercial cytotoxic folate-NDC `ELU001` `NCT05001282` terminated early, underscoring the clinical need for predictive biomarker stratification).

Across 2015–2026, the Bradbury Lab engineered a modular library of C′ dot surface chemistries, targeting ligands, radioisotopes, and cleavable drug linkers:
- **Peptide & Small-Molecule Ligands:**
  - **$\alpha$-MSH (rhenium-cyclized melanocortin-1 receptor `MC1R` agonist):** Targets human (`M21`) and murine (`B16-F10`, `B16-GM`) melanomas ([Kim et al., *Nat. Nanotechnol.* 2016](https://doi.org/10.1038/nnano.2016.164); [Chen et al., *ACS Appl. Mater. Interfaces* 2018](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.7b14362); [De Leon et al., *Nat. Nanotechnol.* 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41565-025-02083-z)).
  - **Cyclic `cRGDY` ($\alpha_v\beta_3/\alpha_v\beta_5$ integrins):** Penetrates blood–brain and intratumoral stromal barriers in PDGFB-driven high-grade glioblastoma (`mGBM`), melanoma, and NSCLC, modulating FAK/Src/ERK signaling at high concentrations ([Benezra et al., *Small* 2015](https://doi.org/10.1002/smll.201402331); [Chen et al., *Sci. Adv.* 2019](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aax5208); [Juthani et al., *Clin. Cancer Res.* 2020](https://doi.org/10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-19-1834)).
  - **`PSMAi` (`Glu–urea–Lys` pharmacophore):** First- and second-generation `PSMAi–PEG2–C′ dots` targeting PSMA (`FOLH1`) in human (`LNCaP`) and murine MYC-driven (`Myc-CaP`, `Hi-Myc`) prostate cancers ([Chen et al., *ACS Appl. Mater. Interfaces* 2019](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.9b15195); [Siddiqui et al., *Cancer Res.* 2026](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-25-4954)).
  - **Folate Receptor-$\alpha$ (`FR-α` / `FOLR1`):** Cathepsin-B-cleavable nanoparticle–drug conjugates (NDCs) for ovarian/breast/lung carcinomas ([Wu et al., *ACS Nano* 2022](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsnano.2c05342)).
- **Engineered Biologics (`scFv` & Designed Ankyrin Repeat Proteins / DARPins):**
  - Site-specifically conjugated **anti-HER2 `scFv–C′ dots`** (<8 nm, preserving renal clearance) for breast imaging ([Chen et al., *Nat. Commun.* 2018](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-06271-5)) and **"hit-and-run" topoisomerase-inhibitor drug-immune conjugates** achieving complete eradication of HER2+ gastric xenografts ([Zhang et al., *Adv. Ther.* 2023](https://doi.org/10.1002/adtp.202200209)).
  - **Bispecific `HER2×VEGF` DARPins** for targeted nanobiotechnology ([Chen et al., *J. Nanobiotechnol.* 2025](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12951-025-03631-4)) and generative scaffold-constrained DARPin design (`DARPinMPNN` targeting mesothelin, VEGF-A, and HER2 with Olivier Elemento; [Pourbaghi, Elemento & Bradbury, *bioRxiv* 2026, `10.64898/2026.08.07.743551`](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.08.07.743551)).
- **Radiolabeled Theranostics & Cytotoxic NDCs:**
  - Diagnostic PET isotopes ($^{89}\text{Zr-DFO}$, $^{124}\text{I}$) and therapeutic $\beta$-/$a$-emitters ($^{177}\text{Lu-DOTA-}\alpha\text{MSH-C′ dots}$, [Zhang et al., *Biomaterials* 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biomaterials.2020.119858); $^{225}\text{Ac-}\alpha\text{MSH-C′ dots}$ inducing local immune transcriptomic remodeling in `B16-F10` melanoma, [Urbanska et al., *Cancer Biother. Radiopharm.* 2020](https://doi.org/10.1089/cbr.2019.3150)).
  - Cleavable small-molecule NDCs carrying **dasatinib** ([Juthani et al., *Clin. Cancer Res.* 2020](https://doi.org/10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-19-1834)), **gefitinib/TKI analogs** in NSCLC ([Madajewski et al., *Clin. Cancer Res.* 2020](https://doi.org/10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-20-0851)), and **doxorubicin** in high-grade glioma ([Aragon-Sanabria et al., *Clin. Cancer Res.* 2022](https://doi.org/10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-21-4053)).

1. **Multi-Condition Cell-Line Viability & Rescue Matrices:**
   - *Bradbury Assay:* Human & murine solid tumor panels (`786-O` ccRCC, `HT-1080` fibrosarcoma, `M21` / `B16-F10` / `B16-GM` melanoma, `Myc-CaP` / `LNCaP` / `PC-3` prostate, `mGBM` glioma, `BxPC3` PDAC, `H1650` NSCLC, `MDA-MB-468` / `MCF7` / `MCF10A` breast) exposed across dose–time grids (1–60 $\mu$M C′ dots, 24–72 h) crossed with **media nutrient states** (complete vs. amino-acid-free vs. cystine-replete vs. dialyzed FBS $\pm$ $\text{FeCl}_3$/FAC) and **orthogonal pharmacological rescues** (`Liproxstatin-1` 1 $\mu$M, `Ferrostatin-1` 1 $\mu$M, `DFO` 100–200 $\mu$M, `Concanamycin A` 100 nM, `zVAD-fmk`, `Nec-1s`, `TLR7/8` blocker, CRISPR `STING1-KO`).

2. **Bulk Transcriptomics of In Vitro Perturbations & Fresh/FFPE Excised Tumors:**
   - *Bradbury Assay:* Agilent SureSelect XT / Illumina bulk RNA-seq (`GPL34290`) on 24–72 h C′ dot-treated cells (`B16-F10`, `B16-GM`, `Myc-CaP`, `LNCaP`) and excised murine FFPE/fresh tumors (`GSE319397`, `GSE319419`: Vehicle, C′ dots alone, ICB, Dual Tx, Triple Tx at day 4 post-last dose).

### 2.2 The Six Exact Fatal Flaws Existing Models Fail to Solve

As underscored by the 2025 international ferroptosis consensus guidelines ([Mishima et al., *Nat. Rev. Mol. Cell Biol.* 2025, `10.1038/s41580-025-00843-2`](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41580-025-00843-2)), any ML model claiming to predict ferroptosis from conventional transcriptomic or 2D chemical features alone fails for six rigorous mechanistic reasons:

1. **Fatal Flaw #1 — Transcriptional Abundance $\neq$ Lipid Peroxidation Flux (and Sign-Inversion Confounding):**
   Ferroptosis is executed by a rapid biochemical chain reaction (Fenton initiation $\rightarrow$ phospholipid peroxyl radical propagation $\rightarrow$ nanopore formation), not by a dedicated transcriptional cascade. Worse, canonical FerrDb "markers" (`SLC7A11`, `FTL`, `FTH1`, `HMOX1`, `NQO1`, `CHAC1`, `PTGS2`) are direct transcriptional targets of **NRF2 (`NFE2L2`)** and **ATF4**. Consequently, *high mRNA expression* of `SLC7A11`, `FTH1`, or `GCLC` in an unperturbed tumor signifies **constitutive NRF2 activation and profound ferroptosis resistance**, whereas *acute transcriptional induction* of `CHAC1`, `PTGS2`, and `SLC7A11` at 12–24 h post-treatment signifies **adaptive ER/oxidative stress during ferroptosis execution**. Unweighted or linear gene-set scorers conflate pre-existing resistance with active death!
2. **Fatal Flaw #2 — Blindness to Membrane Phospholipid Stoichiometry (`di-PUFA-PL` vs. `MUFA-PL` & Ether Lipids):**
   Peroxidation propagation rate scales nonlinearly with the local membrane mole fraction of bis-allylic polyunsaturated phospholipids — specifically **di-polyunsaturated phosphatidylcholines (`PC-PUFA2`)** ([Qiu et al., *Cell* 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2024.01.030)), arachidonoyl/adrenoyl phosphatidylethanolamines (`PE-18:0/20:4`, `PE-18:0/22:4`, [Kagan et al., *Nat. Chem. Biol.* 2017](https://doi.org/10.1038/nchembio.2238)), and peroxisomal PUFA-ether plasmalogens ([Zou et al., *Nature* 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2732-8)) — Divided by protective monounsaturated phospholipids (`MUFA-PLs`) remodeled via `SCD1`, `ACSL3`, and `MBOAT1/2` ([Liang et al., *Cell* 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2023.05.003)). Standard mRNA models neither enforce metabolic flux balance across acyl-CoA remodeling enzymes nor account for exogenous lipid/lipoprotein uptake ([Calhoon et al., *Nature* 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09162-0)).
3. **Fatal Flaw #3 — Blindness to Subcellular Labile $\text{Fe}^{2+}$, Polyamine Iron Buffering & Endogenous Small-Molecule RTAs:**
   Fenton initiation depends on the compartmentalized **lysosomal and cytosolic labile $\text{Fe}^{2+}$ pool** — dynamically buffered by ferritinophagy (`NCOA4`) and newly discovered **endogenous polyamine iron chelators (`spermine`/`spermidine`)** ([Li et al., *Nature* 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10597-2); [Sharma et al., *Cell* 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.07.040)) — pitted against four small-molecule RTA/thiol pools (`GSH`, `CoQ10H2`/vitamin K hydroquinone, `BH4`, and `7-dehydrocholesterol`). Transcriptomic classifiers lack any stoichiometric conservation law connecting enzyme levels to steady-state metabolite concentrations.
4. **Fatal Flaw #4 — Selenoprotein Translational Stalling & Post-Translational Perturbation Blindness:**
   `GPX4` carries an essential catalytic **selenocysteine (`Sec46`)** encoded by an internal `UGA` stop codon ([Ingold et al., *Cell* 2018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2017.11.048)). Decoding `UGA` requires `LRP8` (`ApoER2`)-mediated `SELENOP` uptake, `PSTK`, `SEPSECS`, `EEFSEC`, `SECISBP2`, and mevalonate-pathway isopentenylation of $\text{tRNA}^{[\text{Ser}]\text{Sec}}$. Depletion of selenium or `LRP8` causes **ribosome stalling and collision at the *GPX4* UGA codon**, collapsing functional GPX4 protein while leaving *GPX4* mRNA unchanged or elevated ([Li et al., *Nat. Chem. Biol.* 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41589-022-01033-3)). Similarly, covalent GPX4 inhibitors (`RSL3`, `ML162`), GPX4 degraders (`FIN56`), iron oxidizers (`FINO2`), FSP1 phase-separators (`icFSP1`), and GPX4 `PRMT5` methylation ([Fan et al., *Nat. Cell Biol.* 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41556-025-01610-3)) act post-translationally.
5. **Fatal Flaw #5 — Batch Fragility, Single-Cell Lytic Dropout & Circular Surrogate Labels:**
   LASSO signatures selected on log-normalized TPMs collapse under cross-platform shifts (poly-A fresh RNA-seq vs. Bradbury's FFPE SureSelect RNA-seq or 10x Chromium UMI data). In scRNA-seq, cells undergoing terminal lytic ferroptosis ([Das et al., *Dev. Cell* 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.devcel.2026.01.014)) leak cytosolic mRNA and are discarded by mitochondrial-% QC filters, leaving only pre-ferroptotic survivors or resistant bystanders.
6. **Fatal Flaw #6 — Inability to Generalize Zero-Shot to Chemical, Nutrient & Nanoparticle Perturbations:**
   No published model unifies **cell state + extracellular microenvironment (cystine/AA/selenium/iron/lipids) + perturbation physics (small-molecule target/RTA reactivity OR nanoparticle size, surface $\text{Fe}^{3+}$ chelation, receptor targeting ligand, lysosomal trafficking)**. Hence, every existing ML model is structurally incapable of predicting C′ dot responses in the Bradbury Lab.

### 3.1 Canonical `SLC7A11–GSH–GPX4` Axis & 2025–2026 Structural/Organellar Redefinitions
- **Plasma-Membrane vs. Lysosomal `SLC7A11`:** Beyond importing cystine via system $x_c^-$ (`SLC7A11`/`SLC3A2`) to sustain `GCLC`/`GSS`-mediated GSH synthesis ([Dixon et al., *Cell* 2012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2012.03.042); [Yang et al., *Cell* 2014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2013.12.010)), **Zhou et al. (*Cell* 2025, [`10.1016/j.cell.2025.04.004`](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2025.04.004))** discovered that `SLC7A11` also functions as an **unconventional $\text{H}^+$ transporter on lysosomes**, directly coupling cystine/glutamate homeostasis to lysosomal pH regulation!
- **GPX4 Membrane Fin-Loop & Selenocysteine Control:** **Lorenz et al. (*Cell* Jan 2026, [`10.1016/j.cell.2025.11.014`](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2025.11.014))** resolved a specialized **membrane-anchoring fin-loop structure in GPX4** essential for docking onto peroxidized phospholipid bilayers and preventing neurodegeneration/ferroptosis, complementing selenocysteine catalytic resistance to irreversible overoxidation ([Ingold et al., *Cell* 2018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2017.11.048)) and `LRP8`-dependent selenoprotein translation ([Li et al., *Nat. Chem. Biol.* 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41589-022-01033-3)). Additionally, **Xia et al. (*Cell* Apr 2026, [`10.1016/j.cell.2026.01.009`](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.01.009))** identified a noncanonical in vivo `GPX1–OSBPL8` ferroptosis defense axis in tumors.

### 3.2 Orthogonal Radical-Trapping Systems: `FSP1–CoQ10/Vitamin K`, `GCH1–BH4` & the `DHODH` Controversy
- **`FSP1` (`AIFM2`) Myristoylation, Vitamin K Cycle & Phase Separation:** Myristoylated `FSP1` reduces ubiquinone ($\text{CoQ}_{10}$) and **vitamin K** (phylloquinone/menaquinone) to radical-trapping hydroquinones at the plasma and lysosomal membranes independently of GSH/GPX4 ([Doll et al., *Nature* 2019](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1707-0); [Bersuker et al., *Nature* 2019](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1705-2); [Mishima et al., *Nature* 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05022-3)). Next-generation inhibitor `icFSP1` does not merely block catalysis; it triggers **liquid–liquid phase separation (LLPS)** and membrane detachment of human FSP1 ([Nakamura et al., *Nature* 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06255-6)). Critically, **Wu et al. (*Nature* Jan 2026, [`10.1038/s41586-025-09710-8`](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09710-8))** demonstrated that while FSP1 is often dispensable in standard 2D culture, it is **strictly required in vivo** to suppress ferroptosis and sustain lung adenocarcinoma progression under physiological oxidative stress!
- **`GCH1–BH4` Dual Shield:** GTP cyclohydrolase-1 (`GCH1`) synthesizes tetrahydrobiopterin ($\text{BH}_4$), which acts both as a direct lipophilic RTA and selectively protects two-tail PUFA phosphatidylcholines while increasing $\text{CoQ}_{10}$ ([Kraft et al., *ACS Cent. Sci.* 2020](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscentsci.9b01063); [Soula et al., *Nat. Chem. Biol.* 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41589-020-0613-y)).
- **Critical Nuance on `DHODH` (Scite Contradiction Audit):** While [Mao et al. (*Nature* 2021, `10.1038/s41586-021-03539-7`)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03539-7) proposed inner-mitochondrial `DHODH` as a major GPX4-parallel defense based on high-dose brequinar sensitization, **[Mishima et al. (*Nature* 2023, `10.1038/s41586-023-06269-0`)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06269-0)** proved that high-micromolar brequinar potently inhibits **FSP1** off-target ($\text{IC}_{50} \approx 3\text{–}10\ \mu\text{M}$), whereas selective nanomolar DHODH blockade primarily depletes pyrimidines with only minor mitochondrial ferroptosis effects unless both GPX4 and FSP1 are compromised. Our mechanistic layer must weight `FSP1` far above `DHODH` and model drug polypharmacology!

### 3.3 Membrane Phospholipid Remodeling: `PC-PUFA2`, Ether Plasmalogens & Sex-Hormone `MBOAT1/2`
- **Pro-Ferroptotic Acyl Incorporation & `di-PUFA` Initiation:** Canonical `ACSL4` and `LPCAT3` esterify arachidonic (20:4) and adrenic (22:4) acids into `PE` phospholipids ([Doll et al., *Nat. Chem. Biol.* 2017](https://doi.org/10.1038/nchembio.2239); [Kagan et al., *Nat. Chem. Biol.* 2017](https://doi.org/10.1038/nchembio.2238)), supplemented by peroxisomal `FAR1`/`AGPS`-synthesized **PUFA-ether lipids (plasmalogens)** ([Zou et al., *Nature* 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2732-8)). Extending this paradigm, **Qiu et al. (*Cell* 2024, [`10.1016/j.cell.2024.01.030`](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2024.01.030))** discovered that rare **phosphatidylcholines bearing two polyunsaturated fatty acyl tails (`PC-PUFA2`)** accumulate upon free fatty acid uptake, interact with the mitochondrial electron transport chain to ignite mitochondrial ROS, and initiate ER/plasma-membrane ferroptosis!
- **Anti-Ferroptotic `MBOAT1/2` & Sex Hormones:** **[Liang et al. (*Cell* 2023, `10.1016/j.cell.2023.05.003`)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2023.05.003)** identified `MBOAT1` (transcriptionally driven by **Estrogen Receptor `ESR1`**) and `MBOAT2` (driven by **Androgen Receptor `AR`**) as potent GPX4- and FSP1-independent suppressors that selectively remodel phospholipids toward `PE-MUFA` at the expense of `PE-PUFA`. Furthermore, **Tonnus et al. (*Nature* Sep 2025, [`10.1038/s41586-025-09389-x`](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09389-x))** demonstrated that $17\beta$-oestradiol suppresses ferroptosis via both direct RTA activity and genomic hydropersulfide generation. *(Note the direct relevance to Bradbury's prostate cancer models: `AR` blockade downregulates `MBOAT2`, synergizing with `PSMAi–C′ dots`!).*
- **Extracellular Lipoprotein Shielding:** **Calhoon et al. (*Nature* Aug 2025, [`10.1038/s41586-025-09162-0`](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09162-0))** revealed that tumor sulfated **glycosaminoglycans (GAGs)** drive uptake of circulating lipoproteins carrying $\alpha$-tocopherol and $\text{CoQ}_{10}$ to shield solid tumors from ferroptosis in vivo.

### 3.4 Distal Cholesterol Synthesis & Endogenous `7-Dehydrocholesterol (7-DHC)` Radical Shielding
- Back-to-back *Nature* studies by **[Freitas et al. (*Nature* Feb 2024, `10.1038/s41586-023-06878-9`)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06878-9)** and **[Li et al. (*Nature* Feb 2024, `10.1038/s41586-023-06983-9`)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06983-9)** uncovered that **7-dehydrocholesterol (`7-DHC`)** — synthesized via `SC5D`/`EBP` and cleared to cholesterol by **`DHCR7`** — is an extraordinary endogenous membrane RTA whose conjugated 5,7-diene B-ring reacts with phospholipid peroxyl radicals far faster than PUFA acyl chains, diverting oxidation away from phospholipids and preventing membrane fragmentation.
- **Opposing Enzyme Polarity (Crucial Test for Any ML Model!):** Upstream sterol inhibition (`EBP`, `MSMO1`, `CYP51A1`, `SC5D`) **depletes `7-DHC` and sensitizes** cells to ferroptosis, whereas downstream **`DHCR7` loss/inhibition accumulates `7-DHC` and confers potent ferroptosis resistance** (as seen in Burkitt lymphoma and neuroblastoma). Strikingly, in [Siddiqui et al. (*Cancer Res.* Aug 2026)](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-25-4954), `PSMAi–C′ dots` transcriptionally **suppressed steroid/cholesterol biosynthesis** in `Myc-CaP` prostate tumors, removing endogenous sterol protection during C′ dot iron loading!

### 3.5 Organellar Hotspots: Lysosomal Iron, Lysosome Rupture, ER–Mitochondria Contacts & Polyamine Buffering

### 3.6 Multicellular Spatiotemporal "Trigger Waves" & Nematic Tissue Self-Organization
