## 1. Workstream 1: Permissive Experimental Cis-Regulatory & Epigenomic Datasets (`CisRegPriorSlot`)

### 1.1 Architectural Role in `FerroFlux-FM`
In `FerroFlux-FM`, baseline transcriptomics alone cannot distinguish whether low mRNA of a ferroptosis gatekeeper (*ACSL4*, *FSP1/AIFM2*, *SLC7A11*, *PROM2*, *GCH1*, *ALOX15*, or *TMEM173/STING1*) reflects **transient transcriptional quiescence** (rapidly inducible upon `NFE2L2`, `YAP/TAZ`, `ATF4`, or `IFN-I/TLR` stimulation) versus **irreversible epigenetic lock-in** (CpG island hypermethylation, `H3K27me3`/`H3K9me3` heterochromatin, enhancer deletion, or alternative promoter/intron-retention switching). During pretraining, `CisRegPriorSlot` ingests an experimental gene-level regulatory summary vector $\mathbf{c}_{\text{cis},g} \in \mathbb{R}^{32}$ per gene $g$ aggregating:
1. Promoter ($\pm 1\text{ kb}$ TSS) & ABC-linked distal enhancer chromatin accessibility (`TCGA ATAC-seq`, `CATlas` cell-type ATAC, `ENCODE4 Registry-V4` cCRE overlap);
2. Promoter & linked-enhancer CpG methylation $\beta$-values (`CCLE RRBS`, `Sanger CMP 450K/EPIC`, `TCGA PanCanAtlas 450K/27K`, `CCMA EPIC`);
3. Global chromatin PTM state (`CCLE GCP` 42 histone marks);
4. Alternative TSS usage, cassette/intron-retention splicing (`PCAWG` `PromoterActivity` + `PCAWG_PSI`/`JuncBASE`), and cis-regulatory somatic SNV/SV burden (`PCAWG` non-US WGS + `TCGA ATAC Data S10`).

Because $\mathbf{c}_{\text{cis},g}$ is trained with **modality dropout ($p_{\text{drop}} = 0.5$)** on standard physical units (log-normalized ATAC insertion accessibility, CpG methylation fraction $\beta \in [0,1]$, and PSI splicing ratios), `FerroFlux-FM` operates cleanly on RNA-only inputs while optionally accepting zero-shot sequence-predicted cis-regulatory deltas from `alphagenome` `v2.0.0` strictly at downstream non-commercial inference time.

## 2. Workstream 2: 3D Organoid, Patient-Derived Xenograft (PDX) & Primary Patient Drug-Response Atlases

### 2.1 Why 3D, PDX & Primary Patient Atlases Are Essential for `FerroFlux-FM`
Training solely on 2D immortalized cell-line viability screens (`CTRPv2`, `PRISM`) creates systematic out-of-distribution failure modes when translating to *in vivo* solid tumors (such as PI Bradbury's `786-O`, `HT-1080`, `B16-F10`, `Myc-CaP`, and `Hi-Myc` models):
- **3D Architecture & Cadherin–Hippo–`ACSL4`/`EMP1` Gating:** High-density 3D spheroids and organoids activate E-/N-cadherin–NF2–Hippo signaling, sequestering `YAP`/`TAZ` and suppressing `ACSL4`/`TFRC` in outer proliferative shells while creating hypoxic, lipid-peroxidation-primed inner cores and contiguous cell–cell junctions capable of propagating **osmotic ferroptotic trigger waves** ([Riegman et al., *Nat. Cell Biol.* 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41556-020-0565-1)).
- **Primary Hematopoietic & Pediatric `MYCN`-Driven Hypersensitivity:** Primary *ex vivo* AML blasts (`BeatAML`) and pediatric `MYCN`-amplified neuroblastoma, rhabdomyosarcoma, and H3K27M diffuse midline glioma neurospheres (`CCMA` + `Pediatric DepMap`) exhibit unadapted primary redox physiology — notably **acute `MYCN`-driven cysteine addiction, upregulated `TFRC` labile iron import, and synthetic lethality to `GPX4`/`SLC7A11`/sterol inhibition** ([Floros et al., *Cancer Res.* 2021](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-20-1641); [Lu et al., *Cell Death Dis.* 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41419-021-03790-w); [Alborzinia et al., *Nat. Cancer* 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43018-022-00355-4); [Dächert et al., *Int. J. Cancer* 2020](https://doi.org/10.1002/ijc.32496)).
